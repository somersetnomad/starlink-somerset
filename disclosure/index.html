Referral disclosure

How the Starlink referral links on Rural Signal work and what the author receives.

Some links on this site marked as referral links are my personal Starlink referral link. If you’re new to Starlink and order through it, Starlink gives both of us a reward. At the time of writing that is £40 each, under Starlink’s own terms, which can change.

That’s the only way this site earns anything. Starlink doesn’t pay me to write, doesn’t see guides before they’re published, and has no say over what they say.

Using the link doesn’t change the price you pay. You don’t have to use it: everything on this site is just as useful if you order directly or not at all.

Rural Signal is independent and not affiliated with, endorsed by, or acting for Starlink or SpaceX.
